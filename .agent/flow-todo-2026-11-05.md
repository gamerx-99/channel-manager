# Flow and todo by Part (2026-11-05)

Source: the system review of 2026-11-05 (booking system, room management, finance, people), judged by the four tests in `DECISIONS.md`:
1. a guest can book through the hotel's web page; 2. the back office can check every booking and payment; 3. the accounts are correct; 4. staff work is controlled and runs smoothly.

hayhost is a hotel website and direct-booking service with a simple back office, not a channel manager. Add detail only when a real hotel asks. Each hotel decides its own policies.

## How Parts work together

- A Part owns a fixed list of files. Two Parts that can run at the same time never own the same file.
- A Part that needs a file another Part owns waits until that Part is merged ("after").
- Shared files (`en.ts`, `th.ts`, `.agent/handoff.md`): change only your own keys and your own entry. Never `git add -A`; add named files.
- Every Part ends with `pnpm check`, the **whole** `pnpm test` and `pnpm build` passing (paste the output), a handoff entry, Thai wording tables for new or changed Thai text, and a pushed branch. Nobody pushes `main` or deploys except with the owner's go-ahead in chat.
- A change that needs a database migration: write the migration file, never apply it. A human applies it, and the Part stays open until then.

## Parts

### Part 1: finance correctness (Claude; done locally, awaiting push and a live look)
Test 3. Audit of every money figure on the Dashboard and the financial report found and fixed:
- Revenue, arrivals and departures counted only `confirmed`; checked-in and checked-out guests vanished and "departures today" was nearly always 0.
- The report counted everything except `cancelled` as revenue (held, awaiting payment, expired, rejected, refunded).
- "Today" and the 30-day period used the UTC day, not the Thai day (wrong between 00:00 and 07:00).
- "Rooms free today" came from `rooms.status`, which nothing updates, so it always showed every room free.
- A walk-in paid at the counter lost its payment method and showed as outstanding.

Owns: `shared/finance.ts` (+test), `server/financials.ts` (+test), `server/d1/dashboardStats.ts` (+test), `server/routers/dashboardRouter.ts`, `server/routerUtils.ts` (`todayIso`), `client/src/pages/Home.tsx`, the `isoToday` helper in `DashboardCalendarView.tsx`, and the staff-insert payment columns in `server/d1/booking.ts` (+ its test).
Definition of revenue (for the record): value of `confirmed`, `checked_in` and `checked_out` bookings by check-in date, Thai time. It is not cash received; cash by payment date needs a payment date on every booking (a decision, below).
Open: owner confirms the definition; someone looks at the Dashboard signed in; push and deploy.
