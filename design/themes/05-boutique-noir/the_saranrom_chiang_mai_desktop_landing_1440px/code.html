I. Sanctuary / ปรัชญาแห่งการพักผ่อน

A timeless retreat sheltered in teak and silent courtyards.

T

welve suites behind a colonial-era facade inside the old city walls. Private, unhurried, deliberately small. Designed for unhurried contemplation in the heart of Chiang Mai's historic quarter. Here, the passage of morning shadow over hand-chiselled pillars marks the only schedule of importance.

Crafted during the zenith of northern Lanna timber architecture, every corridor retains its aged teakwood patina, paired with fragrant night-blooming jasmine, unadorned brass fixtures, and filtered temple bells from neighboring Phra Sing sanctuary.
