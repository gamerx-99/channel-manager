## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the sensory intimacy of an old-world northern Thai sanctuary bathed in nocturnal elegance. It marries high-end editorial couture with architectural quietude—evoking teakwood verandahs in lamplight, hand-hammered temple gilding, and shadowed courtyard pools under an ink-black sky.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** Discerning global travelers, cultural connoisseurs, design pilgrims, and private luxury retreat seekers seeking an authentic, slow-paced sanctuary within Chiang Mai’s historic moat.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Intimate, tranquil, reverent, and deeply cinematic. The interface should feel less like transactional software and more like a hardbound, cloth-wrapped monograph or a private guestbook invitation.

### Design Movement
**Minimalist Editorial Noir** with architectural discipline:
- Unhurried, expansive breathing space.
- 1px gold hairline structures reminiscent of traditional gilt woodwork.
- Classical chapter formatting using Roman numerals (`I`, `II`, `III`, `IV`) for suites, sensory rituals, and culinary journeys.
- Subdued, low-frequency atmospheric transitions that preserve quiet luxury rather than digital busyness.

## Colors

The palette is tuned strictly to a dark ambient environment where deep soot and aged espresso absorb light, while antique gold and soft champagne reflect it with deliberate restraint.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Accent (`#B8955A` - Antique Gold):** Applied selectively to interactive moments, focus rings, fine borders, and key brand marks. Maintains a minimum contrast ratio of 4.5:1 against the charcoal base for graphical focal elements.
- **Secondary Dark (`#231C17` - Deep Teak Espresso):** Used for elevated surface planes, contextual cards, drawers, and modal backdrops, evoking hand-polished teak furniture.
- **Neutral Surface (`#141414` - Raw Charcoal):** The foundational ground color. Deep, matte, and light-absorbing to make imagery and gilded borders appear backlit.
- **Primary Text (`#E9DFCF` - Champagne Silk):** High-contrast editorial text, achieving an accessible 11.2:1 contrast ratio against the primary charcoal ground for effortless reading.
- **Secondary Text & Hairlines (`#8C8279` - Weathered Stone):** Used for secondary captions, metadata, understated rules, and inactive borders. Reaches a compliant 4.6:1 contrast ratio against `#141414`.

### Subtle State Tints
- **Hairline Border Base:** `rgba(184, 149, 90, 0.35)` to prevent excessive visual vibration on Retina screens.
- **Hairline Border Highlight:** `#B8955A` for hovered or active states.
- **Surface Hover Overlay:** `rgba(233, 223, 207, 0.03)` on espresso surfaces.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes high-contrast transitional serif headings with an open, highly legible grotesque body.

### Font Pairings & Localization
- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Carries the drama of European editorial fashion magazines while harmonizing with traditional Lanna decorative flourishes. Styled in low weights (`400` and `500`) to remain lithe and crisp. Fallback: *Taviraj*.
- **Body & Functional Labels (`Be Vietnam Pro`):** Clean, geometric, yet possessing humanist warmth. Its modern proportions provide comfortable legibility in low-light dark mode, effortlessly handling bilingual parity across Latin and Southeast Asian phrasing. Fallback: *Bai Jamjuree*.

### Formatting Principles
- **Chapter Markers:** Structural section identifiers utilize `label-numeral` set in Roman numerals (`I.`, `II.`, `III.`, `IV.`) followed by wide letter-spaced `label-caps` in `#B8955A`.
- **Case Rules:** All button labels, navigation links, and kicker badges must be styled in uppercase with a minimum letter spacing of `0.15em` to `0.22em`.
- **Body Copy:** Kept to light weight (`300`) with generous line-heights (`1.6` to `1.7`) to create an airy, uncrowded reading tempo.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors the architectural floor plan of traditional northern Thai teak compounds: symmetrical pavilions, shadowed corridors, and expansive interior verandas.

### Grid & Layout Rules
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column symmetrical fluid grid with `4rem` outer canvas margins and `2rem` gutters. Content blocks prefer offset spans (e.g., 7 columns of cinematic imagery juxtaposed against 4 columns of text, leaving 1 column of pure negative space).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid with `2.5rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4-column single-column flow with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Imagery extends full-bleed across gutters when framed by hairlines.

### Spatial Rhythm
Negative space is treated as a core design material rather than void. Vertical gaps between narrative chapters range between `5rem` and `8rem`, allowing imagery and editorial storytelling to settle without competition. Component-level padding relies strictly on geometric multiples: `space-md` (`1rem`) for inputs and chips, scaling to `space-xl` (`3.5rem`) for luxury booking and suite panels.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than relying on modern floating drop shadows, this design system establishes spatial layers through **Tonal Shading, Architectural Borders, and Atmospheric Vignettes**.

### Elevation Layers
1. **Base Foundation (`#141414`):** Canvas level. Deep matte charcoal.
2. **Surface Tier 1 (`#231C17`):** Elevated cards, booking widgets, and suite overviews. Sits directly above the foundation, bordered by a delicate 1px hairline.
3. **Surface Tier 2 (Floating Modals, Drawers):** `#1E1814` with a subtle backdrop blur (`12px`) and an ambient, low-light radial falloff: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`.

### Border Depth
Depth is demarcated using strict 1px hairlines:
- **Default hairline:** `1px solid rgba(184, 149, 90, 0.3)`.
- **Selected or focused hairline:** `1px solid #B8955A`.
- **Divider rules:** `1px solid rgba(140, 130, 121, 0.2)`.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a strictly **Sharp (0px)** geometry.

- **Architectural Edge:** All containers, buttons, imagery viewports, modal sheets, and inputs carry razor-sharp right angles (`border-radius: 0px`).
- **Rationale:** Reflects the rectilinear lines of woven bamboo screens, polished teak beams, and tailored architectural stone. Avoids the informal or playful posture of rounded corners, preserving an uncompromising, high-fashion attitude.

## Components

### Ghost Buttons (Primary & Secondary)
- **Primary Ghost:** Transparent background with a `1px solid #B8955A` hairline. Label rendered in `label-caps` (`#E9DFCF`, `0.2em` tracking). Padding: `14px 32px`. Hover state: subtle background transition to `rgba(184, 149, 90, 0.08)` and full `#B8955A` text illumination.
- **Solid Accent (Direct Booking/Reserve):** Background `#B8955A` with `#141414` dark text for immediate conversion visibility. Sharp corners, no rounded edges. Hover state: `#C8A86F`.
- **Text Link:** `label-caps` typography underlined with a `1px` gold rule positioned `6px` below text.

### Cards & Suites Showcases
- **Container:** Grounded in `#231C17` espresso tone with a perimeter `1px` border of `rgba(184, 149, 90, 0.35)`.
- **Image Framing:** Edge-to-edge cinematic photography with a dark vignette gradient (`rgba(20, 20, 20, 0) 60%` to `rgba(20, 20, 20, 0.8) 100%`) anchoring the lower boundary.
- **Metadata Integration:** Top left corner anchors a `label-numeral` badge (e.g., `SUITE NO. 04`), styled in `#8C8279`.

### Form Fields & Booking Pickers
- **Text Input / Date Selector:** Minimalist bottom-bordered or boxed hairline container. Background: `rgba(35, 28, 23, 0.6)`. Hairline: `1px solid rgba(140, 130, 121, 0.4)`. Focus state: transitions border color to `#B8955A` with zero glow radius.
- **Floating Labels:** Formatted in `label-caps` (`#8C8279`), shifting to `#B8955A` on focus.
- **Calendar Picker:** Monochromatic charcoal grid with gold hairline borders highlighting selected dates.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Style:** Rectilinear pill-free tags. `1px solid rgba(184, 149, 90, 0.25)`.
- **Active State:** Background `rgba(184, 149, 90, 0.15)` with `1px solid #B8955A`, label in `#E9DFCF`.
- **Inactive State:** Background transparent, label in `#8C8279`.

### Lists & Navigation
- **Editorial Index:** Accordions and navigational list rows separated by horizontal rules (`rgba(140, 130, 121, 0.2)`).
- **Prefixes:** Indexed with Roman numerals (`I`, `II`, `III`) in `Playfair Display` at `14px`, offset from the item title by `space-md`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Shape:** 14px sharp squares (checkbox) or rotated 12px diamond glyphs (radio).
- **Border:** `1px solid #8C8279`.
- **Checked State:** Border turns to `#B8955A` with a solid interior gold square or diamond mark.
