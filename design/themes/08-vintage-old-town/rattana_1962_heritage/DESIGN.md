## Brand & Style

This design system channels the warmth, tactile character, and romantic charm of 1960s Bangkok and provincial Thai travel ephemera. Rooted in mid-century railway travel, heritage shophouse hospitality, and letterpress printing, the visual direction merges postal artifacts with early commercial modernist typography. 

The aesthetic is Tactile & Nostalgic Editorial:
- **Paper & Ink Foundation:** Interfaces are treated as physical stationery—faded parchment, weathered broadsheets, and train passes—grounded with crisp dark ink lines.
- **Graphic Accents:** Ephemera motifs such as stamped validation badges, ticket notch cutouts, perforated coupon dividers, and bordered postcard frames.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Intimate, nostalgic, welcoming, and artisanal. It invites travelers to feel like honored guests stepping into an authentic mid-century family guesthouse.

## Colors

The palette is derived from aged print inks and sun-faded pigment blocks on sun-cured archival pulp:

- **Primary (`#C2562E` - Brick Orange):** The primary call-to-action and identity tone. Reminiscent of baked terracotta roof tiles and traditional red ink stamps.
- **Secondary (`#2E7C7A` - Retro Teal):** Complementary focal points, navigation highlights, badges, and secondary actions inspired by vintage locomotive enamel and painted wooden shutters.
- **Tertiary (`#D9A441` - Faded Mustard):** Accent highlights, status tags, star ratings, and celebratory postal seals.
- **Neutral Dark (`#2B2622` - Dark Ink / Espresso):** Deep woodblock print tone used for body text, crisp structural borders, and line illustrations. Replaces pure black to retain an organic print finish.
- **Neutral Light Spectrum:**
  - Base Surface: `#F1E6CC` (Aged Parchment)
  - Surface Dim: `#E7DAC0` (Subdued Kraft Paper)
  - Surface Bright: `#FAF5E8` (Fine Postcard Cream)
  - Lowest / Contrast White: `#FFFFFF` (Bleached Postage Border)
- **Supporting Mid-Tone (`#8C6A48` - Muted Sienna):** Secondary borders, timestamp labels, subtle dividers, and inactive states.

All foreground and text combinations must strictly adhere to WCAG AA contrast against `#F1E6CC` and `#FAF5E8`.

## Typography

Typography pairs geometric mid-century signage with clean, welcoming text clarity:

- **Headlines & Display:** `Space Grotesk` carries the angular, blocky spirit of 1960s Thai-Western transit signage and vintage commercial lettering. Use uppercase tracking on high-level section anchors and ticket headers.
- **Body & Content:** `Be Vietnam Pro` offers warmth, open apertures, and optimal readability across varied screen densities while echoing classic mid-century grotesque shapes.
- **Accents & Handwritten Annotations:** For postcards, margin notes, and signed desk notes, incorporate an authentic cursive handwriting style with an organic tilt, treated as an editorial decoration layered over the primary grid.
- **Labels & Badges:** `Space Grotesk` set in all-caps with generous letter-spacing evokes vintage rubber stamps, baggage tags, and letterpress date marks.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout follows a modular, tactile grid inspired by newspaper sheets and framed travel journals:

- **Grid Architecture:** 12 columns on desktop (`margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`), transitioning to 6 columns on tablet (`margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`), and 4 columns on mobile (`margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 1rem`).
- **Framed Canvas Margins:** Main layouts are encased within an outer parchment border. The entire screen reads like a physical sheet of paper with deliberate outer margins rather than an edge-to-edge sterile viewport.
- **Rhythm & Padding:** Component internal padding uses the `space-*` scale strictly based on an 8px base rhythm (`0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2.5rem`), maintaining structured visual balance within notched and stamped boundaries.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than relying on modern blurred shadows, elevation simulates paper layering, postal stamps, and pressboard cards:

- **Tonal Stacking:** Surfaces elevate through tonal contrast: Ground (`#F1E6CC`), secondary card container (`#FAF5E8`), inner contrast field (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Hard Cast Shadows:** For elevated elements (cards, ticket stubs, modal sheets), use crisp, offset, non-diffused or low-diffusion ink shadows:
  - `box-shadow: 3px 3px 0px 0px #2B2622` (Standard tactile depth)
  - `box-shadow: 5px 5px 0px 0px #2B2622` (Hovered or floating depth)
- **Framed Inset & Borders:** Structural containers use a solid `1.5px` or `2px` border in `#2B2622` or `#8C6A48`. Perforations and coupon sections are indicated via dashed border lines (`border: 2px dashed #8C6A48`).
- **Stamp Elevation:** Circular validation seals ("SINCE 1962", "ROOM CONFIRMED") sit flush with a subtle rotational offset (-3deg to 4deg) and a slightly distressed multiply-blend aesthetic.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a strict `0` (Sharp) baseline, customized with vintage mechanical die-cut details:

- **Notched Corners:** Action cards, coupon vouchers, and primary ticket buttons incorporate notched or chamfered 45-degree corners (clipped via CSS `clip-path` or SVG masks) to simulate clipped railway tickets and luggage tags.
- **Postcard Borders:** Content images and featured room previews are mounted with uniform white borders (`#FFFFFF` 8px padding) to reflect 1960s snapshot prints.
- **Circular Seals:** Stamped badges and decorative seals are 100% circular with double concentric ring strokes, breaking the sharp geometry of grid containers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Ticket Button):** Brick orange (`#C2562E`) background, cream (`#FAF5E8`) bold uppercase label, 2px `#2B2622` outline, notched corners (6px chamfer). Casts a 3px hard ink shadow (`#2B2622`). On active press, the button shifts `translate(2px, 2px)` with shadow reduced to 1px.
- **Secondary:** Retro teal (`#2E7C7A`) surface, parchment label, 2px `#2B2622` outline.
- **Tertiary / Link:** `#2B2622` label with a bottom dashed underline in `#C2562E`.

### Cards & Ticket Containers
- **Postcard Card:** Off-white `#FAF5E8` background, 2px solid `#2B2622` border, 3px 3px ink drop shadow. Displays room photography with an authentic white photo border framing.
- **Boarding Pass / Voucher Card:** Two-part card split by a vertical or horizontal dashed line (`border-style: dashed; border-color: #8C6A48;`) with semi-circular notch cutouts on opposite sides to emulate perforated stubs.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips:** Rectangular, sharp corners, bordered with 1.5px `#2B2622`. Filled with `#F1E6CC` or `#D9A441`.
- **Stamp Badges:** Circular layout with circular text path or stacked center text ("SINCE 1962", "RATTANA GH"). Rendered in Retro Teal or Brick Orange with double hairline circles.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- **Fields:** Cream background (`#FAF5E8`), 1.5px `#2B2622` border, sharp corners. Placeholder text in `#8C6A48`. Focused state thickens the border to 2px in `#2E7C7A` without modern halo glows.
- **Labels:** Set in uppercase `label-md` (`Space Grotesk`) directly above the input, accompanied by a small vintage index symbol or number (`No. 01`, `DATE:`).

### Selection Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp square boxes and diamond-angled radio indicators with 2px `#2B2622` outlines. Selected states fill with `#C2562E` featuring a bold cream check or central dot.

### Lists & Tables
- **Guest Registers & Price Lists:** Tabular data separated by single-pixel horizontal ruled lines (`#8C6A48` at 50% opacity). Alternating rows can take a faint wash of `#E7DAC0`.
