## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the serene, historic craftsmanship of boutique hospitality in Lamphun, Northern Thailand. It balances the tactile permanence of centuries-old carved teakwood (*mai sak*) with the unhurried warmth of an intimate family estate. The aesthetic blends **Tactile Heritage Craft** with **Refined Editorial Hospitality**: generous spatial framing, archival serif typography, architectural arch motifs, and gold leaf accents reminiscent of temple frescoes.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** Cultural travelers, heritage architecture enthusiasts, couples, and retreat seekers seeking authentic, slow-living luxury away from urban mass tourism.
- **Emotional Response:** Grounded calm, sensory warmth, generational hospitality, craftsmanship, and timeless provenance.
- **Visual Tenets:**
  - *Architectural Framing:* Rounded-arch tops (*singha* and temple window lintels) applied to featured imagery and primary cards.
  - *Filigree Precision:* Fine line work and delicate ornamental accents referencing northern Thai woodcarving and gilt lacquer (*lai rot nam*).
  - *Heirloom Materiality:* Deep wood pigments set against raw parchment and earthen cream backdrops rather than clinical cold whites.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from traditional Lanna material culture: aged golden teakwood, cinnabar lacquerware (*khrueang khoen*), gold leaf illumination, and hand-beaten mulberry parchment (*saa* paper).

### Semantic Palette & Roles
- **Primary Teak (`#5B3A21`):** Represents structural timber and master branding. Used for top-level headers, primary brand marks, and prominent container frames.
- **Accent Lacquer Red (`#9E2B25`):** Reserved for primary interactive touchpoints, booking call-to-actions, badge alerts, and seasonal offers. Contrast against `#FAF6EF` and `#F4ECDD` exceeds 5.2:1 (exceeds WCAG AA).
- **Highlight Gold Leaf (`#C9A45C`):** Used strictly for ornamental accents, star ratings, filigree line delimiters, and sub-tier badges. For text, pair strictly against deep ink backgrounds.
- **Deep Ink Neutral (`#2A211B`):** The primary reading ink. Delivers a minimum 12.8:1 contrast ratio over base cream backdrops for long-form comfort.
- **Background Warm Cream (`#F4ECDD`):** Global canvas background setting the soft, tactile undertone of unbleached cloth.
- **Surface Cream (`#FAF6EF`):** Elevated card surface, modal surface, and form input container.
- **Surface Container Deep (`#EFE6D5`):** Recessed surfaces, segmented control tracks, borders, and image backplates.

### Contrast Compliance Rules
- White text (`#FFFFFF`) is permitted exclusively over `#5B3A21` and `#9E2B25` where contrast measures 6.8:1 and 4.7:1 respectively.
- For fine body text and metadata labels, use `#2A211B` over `#FAF6EF` and `#F4ECDD`.
- `#C9A45C` is treated as a graphic metal tone; avoid placing gold-leaf text over light cream backgrounds.

## Typography

The typographical pairing reflects northern Thai manuscript refinement married with pristine digital clarity.

- **Headline Family:** `Noto Serif` (with fallback to `Noto Serif Thai` for bilingual Thai-English implementation). Characterized by sculpted serifs and high elegance, bringing the quiet dignity of classical Lanna literature.
- **Body & Label Family:** `Be Vietnam Pro` (with fallback to `Sarabun` in bilingual production). Provides exceptional legibility, organic loop stems, and harmonious balance beside serif titles.

### Hierarchy & Letter Spacing
- Uppercase tracking is applied to `label-*` tokens (`0.06em` to `0.12em`) to evoke classical architectural signage and brass door plaques.
- Body paragraphs must strictly maintain a 1.6 to 1.7 line height ratio to guarantee reading serenity over natural paper tones.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy mirrors the proportions of classic teak compounds (*huan lanna*): generous veranda thresholds, deliberate symmetry, and clear pauses between structural bays.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid, `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Max container bound of `1280px` centered to maintain intimate editorial intimacy.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column grid, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.25rem`.
- **Mobile (Up to 767px):** 4-column fluid grid, `margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 1rem`.

### Vertical Rhythms & Delimiters
- Editorial sections use `space-2xl` (64px) on desktop and `space-xl` (40px) on mobile.
- Section boundaries frequently feature hairline dividers with a central decorative gold rosette or lotus node.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than relying on modern synthetic drop shadows, this design system achieves depth through **physical layering, warm ambient occlusions, and subtle gilded containment**.

### Elevation Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Base Ground):** `#F4ECDD` canvas representing unpolished teak verandas and earthen ground.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Cards & Panels):** `#FAF6EF` with a 1px border of `#EFE6D5` or `#C9A45C` (at 40% alpha). Shadow is soft, diffused, and warm: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(42, 33, 27, 0.06)`.
- **Tier 2 (Floating Overlays & Popovers):** `#FAF6EF` framed with a dual border: an outer 1px `#5B3A21` at 20% opacity and an inner 1px border of `#FAF6EF`. Shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(42, 33, 27, 0.12)`.
- **Tier 3 (Booking Drawers & Modal Sheets):** Backdrop tinted in `#2A211B` at 65% opacity. Modals use sharp internal containment with warm directional occlusion: `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(42, 33, 27, 0.22)`.

## Shapes

The shape system expresses a dual identity: crisp, handcrafted joinery for interactive utility, juxtaposed with traditional temple arches for imagery and showcase containers.

### Edge Geometries
- **Micro UI & Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Badges, Tabs):** Strict 4px border-radius (`roundedness: 1`), referencing the precise mortise-and-tenon woodworking of northern carpenter guilds.
- **Card Containers:** 4px radius on bottom corners, or fully boxed.
- **The Lanna Arch Mask (`shape-arch-top`):** Applied exclusively to hero media, room photography banners, and specialty feature cards. The top edge features an arched profile (`border-top-left-radius: 120px; border-top-right-radius: 120px; border-bottom-left-radius: 4px; border-bottom-right-radius: 4px`), evoking historic monastery windows (*na tang*).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Reserve / Book Now):** Background solid Lacquer Red (`#9E2B25`), text Warm Cream (`#FAF6EF`), border-radius `4px`. Hover transitions to `#80201B` with an ambient glow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 14px rgba(158, 43, 37, 0.3)`).
- **Secondary Action (Explore Villa / Dining):** Background transparent, 1.5px solid border in Primary Teak (`#5B3A21`), text `#5B3A21`. Hover fills with `#5B3A21` and text transitions to `#FAF6EF`.
- **Tertiary / Link Action:** Text `#9E2B25`, underlined with a 1px Gold Leaf (`#C9A45C`) line offset by 4px.

### Cards & Room Showcases
- **Container Structure:** Background `#FAF6EF`, 1px perimeter border `#EFE6D5`.
- **Image Treatment:** Room hero images employ the `shape-arch-top` frame with an inner 1px inset gold border.
- **Meta Block:** Room specifications (bed size, river view, capacity) are styled in `label-md` with `space-sm` gaps, delimited by small gold bullet glyphs (`•`).

### Form Inputs & Date Pickers
- **Text Inputs & Dropdowns:** Background `#FAF6EF`, 1px border `#EFE6D5`, radius `4px`, text `#2A211B`. Focus state transitions the border to `#5B3A21` with a 2px outer ring of `#C9A45C` at 30% opacity.
- **Labels:** Set in `label-md` uppercase, color `#5B3A21`.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes:** 18px square, 3px border-radius. Unchecked border 1.5px `#5B3A21`. Checked background `#9E2B25` with an unbleached cream checkmark icon.
- **Radio Buttons:** 18px circle, unchecked border 1.5px `#5B3A21`. Checked displays an inner concentric 8px disc of `#9E2B25`.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Default State:** Background `#EFE6D5`, text `#5B3A21`, radius `4px`, padding `space-xs space-md`.
- **Selected State:** Background `#5B3A21`, text `#FAF6EF`, radius `4px`.

### Specialized Domain Components
- **Kanok / Filigree Dividers:** A horizontal rule accented with a centralized symmetrical floral motif in `#C9A45C` for separating dining courses or estate amenities.
- **Heritage Badge:** A pill or clipped rectangle badge in `#FAF6EF` with a 1px border of `#C9A45C` and typography in uppercase `label-sm` (`#5B3A21`), used to highlight architectural provenance (e.g., *"EST. 1924"* or *"HAND-CARVED TEAK"*).
