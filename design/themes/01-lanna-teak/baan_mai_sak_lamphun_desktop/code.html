A 90-year-old teak house turned into four quiet rooms, five minutes’ walk from Wat Phra That Hariphunchai.

filter_vintage

Rooted within the ancient walled settlement of Hariphunchai, Baan Mai Sak Lamphun preserves the measured tempo of classical Northern Siamese domestic life. Built from old-growth golden teakwood using traditional mortise-and-tenon joins, the estate breathes naturally with Lamphun's seasonal mountain drafts.

Consciously spared from commercial expansion, our four private guest chambers celebrate handwoven textiles from local cotton weavers, low-lying tea verandas, and sunlit courtyards shaded by century-old frangipani. Here, true hospitality is an unhurried, generous ritual of rest.
